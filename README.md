King Welfare Foundation

About Us

King Welfare Foundation is a charitable welfare initiative dedicated to helping orphans, poor families, widows, and persons with disabilities in Loralai, Balochistan, Pakistan.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support needy people through:

- Food assistance
- Education support
- Healthcare assistance
- Financial assistance
- Support for widows and orphans
- Help for persons with disabilities

Our Vision

To build a better future through compassion, humanity, equality, and community support.

Our Values

- Humanity
- Kindness
- Equality
- Transparency
- Helping those in need
